Free shipping is over $150 to metro only, so ads go where shipping is free or a Specialist Store is close.

- Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Canberra (Meta 50 mile radius; Google 30 to 60 km).
- Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Newcastle, Wollongong.
- 25 km around the regional Specialist Stores: Ballina, Lismore, Batemans Bay, Nowra, Geelong.
- No NT, no Darwin, no Hobart, no other regional. Presence only (people in the area, not "interested in").
- Age suggestion 35 to 64 with Advantage+ audience on.
